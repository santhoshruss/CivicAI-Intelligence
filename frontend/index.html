The Urban Infrastructure Challenge

Why traditional civic reporting systems fail modern cities

⚠️ The Problem

Municipal dispatchers receive hundreds of duplicate, unstructured citizen reports daily. Critical electrical hazards and expanding potholes languish in bureaucratic backlogs while minor complaints clutter maintenance queues.

  • Duplicate submissions waste up to 40% of inspection capacity
  • Lack of objective visual damage severity scoring
  • Disconnected maintenance guidelines and slow work order dispatch

✨ The CivicAI Solution

An integrated intelligence system combining automated image classification, geospatial proximity duplicate clustering, and tool-calling agentic workflows that prepare actionable work orders.

  • Automated visual damage triage across 5 critical civic categories
  • FAISS-powered RAG assistant grounding actions in official city SOPs
  • Human-in-the-loop authorization ensuring complete municipal control

How CivicAI Operates

End-to-end municipal triage and resolution workflow

1

Citizen Reports

Citizen snaps photo and captures exact GPS coordinates of road or utility damage.

2

AI Vision Triage

Computer vision analyzes damage severity and calculates proximity duplicate score.

3

Agentic Work Order

AI agent consults RAG maintenance manuals and stages prioritized draft repair orders.

4

Officer Verification

Authorized officer approves work order and tracks field repair to resolution.
